# Science Model Separation

## Tier A — established / conventional
- Local Newtonian gravity
- Kinematic rigid-body approximations
- Oscillatory pendulum demonstrator
- Geometric optical visualization
- Classical scalar-wave concepts

## Tier B — planned accepted-model solvers
- collision constraints and friction
- heat transfer
- pressure and ideal/real-gas approximations
- reaction kinetics
- circuit solver
- EM field solver
- diffraction/interference solver
- localized quantum experiment solver

## Tier C — research / extreme-regime models
- curved-spacetime ray tracing
- relativistic clocks / geodesics
- semiclassical gravity experiments
- analogue horizon systems

## Tier D — EFMW experimental
Every EFMW module must eventually declare:
- exact equation
- symbols
- units
- assumptions
- numerical method
- boundary conditions
- predicted observable
- control model
- failure criterion

No Tier D result should be relabeled as established physics by the game UI.
